Units 1–5 AI audio: listen-check

Generated with ElevenLabs Eleven v3, using the voice "Ivan – The Educator". Each unit has two takes. Open a take, jump to the time shown for each item, and mark it.

How to judge each item

Take 1 OK / Take 2 OK: the word is clear, the stress is on the marked vowel, and nothing is added or cut off. Mark both if both are good.

Both wrong: wrong stress, wrong word, or a clipped or garbled sound in both takes. These get regenerated.

Unsure: leave these for the tutor.

Orange notes come from the automated timing check. They point to spots worth a closer listen, such as a very short item that may be clipped, or one that runs into the item before it. They are not confirmed errors.

"Stress I added": the course shows this text without a stress mark, so I added one for the script. Check that stress especially.
