## Evidence admission standard

Every linguistic capability admitted from research must retain:

1. An immutable revision or archived identity.
2. Exact retained source bytes.
3. Canonical source URL, author/publisher when available, and immutable identifier.
4. License name, license URL, and retained license evidence.
5. The exact source span supporting the claim.
6. Any transformation, attribution, and per-record hash.
7. Explicit relationships to each instruction, example, practice item, check, answer, distractor, feedback line, and remediation step.

Unacceptable shortcuts include keyword matches, mutable pages, dictionary-row grammar inference, source co-occurrence, invented Russian or stress, unattributed examples, and Penguin instructional content.
