This report is generated from the packaged curriculum, the curriculum validator, the review-packet generator, the source-capability model, and the release gate. It does not approve linguistic content, owned-edition mappings, unit installation, either authority-variance proposal, candidate freeze, physical acceptance, AT-031, or public release. A future independent curriculum audit, including a Claude audit, is not a qualified human signature.

Generator: `app/src/test/java/dev/russiancompanion/v2/audit/CurriculumReadinessReconciliation.kt`.

Invocation: `WRITE_CURRICULUM_READINESS=1 ./gradlew :app:testDebugUnitTest --offline --tests dev.russiancompanion.v2.audit.CurriculumReadinessReconciliationTest`.

Committed source snapshots use `NOT_OBSERVED_IN_SOURCE_SNAPSHOT` for this build-dependent observation; the Android tests inspect the actual merged manifest separately. These are narrow structural or known-marker checks. They are not comprehensive authorship, copyright, or merged-manifest analysis.
