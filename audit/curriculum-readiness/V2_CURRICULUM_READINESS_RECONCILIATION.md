A future independent curriculum audit, including a Claude audit, is not a qualified human signature.

Generator: `app/src/test/java/dev/russiancompanion/v2/audit/CurriculumReadinessReconciliation.kt`.

Committed source snapshots use `NOT_OBSERVED_IN_SOURCE_SNAPSHOT` for this build-dependent observation; the Android tests inspect the actual merged manifest separately. These are narrow structural or known-marker checks. They are not comprehensive authorship, copyright, or merged-manifest analysis.
