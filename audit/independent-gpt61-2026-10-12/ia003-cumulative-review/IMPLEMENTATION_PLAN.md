Baseline is `11d70e5e4512ff93758ebf246f2a4773f83ca0e0`. BASELINE_INVENTORY.json records all thirty actual advancement rules, the current hard-coded matches, recognition objectives/check kinds, and inspected-file SHA256s. ## Authority and observed behavior

Read the complete `docs/governance/MASTER_AUTHORITY_v2.0.md`, especially MA2-022/024/026/027 and MA2-085/086/088. MA2-027 requires previously taught objectives across more than one skill where applicable. MA2-088 requires due cumulative review in addition to instruction, book work and checks. MA2-026 disallows determining curricular ordering from IDs or lesson arithmetic. No authority defines a time-based vocabulary SRS here.

Only Unit2 sets `requireDueReview=true`. `currentUnitRequirements` finds steps by `obj-u2-review` or the substring `review`, then checks completion of those steps. An empty match list succeeds. Switching a Unit3 flag to true therefore cannot implement IA003. Unit2 still has its own useful foundation-review steps: preserve their required checks and ordinary credit, not merely their flag.

`practiceQueue(...,"mixed")` uses current evidence-completed units, including only those in `currentCompletedUnitNumbers`; unlike other labs it does not include an unfinished current unit. It sorts current governed recent misses and then IDs. It can revisit taught material, but creates no due assignment or history separate from optional practice. `prepareGovernedAttempt` and LocalStore's write validation accept only PRESCRIBED_CHECK and OPTIONAL_PRACTICE. Neither role identifies a cumulative assignment. Do not reinterpret existing rows as review completion.

The baseline harness uses the real pack, in-memory Room store, actual governed prescribed writes, validated step-completion digests, book attestations and historical completion rows for Units1/2. It asserts that these are current evidence-completed units before testing Unit3 advancement with no subsequent earlier-unit answer. Book attestations and simulated teaching completion in this test are fixtures, not observed human language performance or device acceptance. The mutated Unit3 flag test builds and records all evidence against that exact mutated pack to avoid a stale-digest false failure.

## Policy to implement, version 1

Declare explicit cumulative-review policies on the applicable gates, rather than deriving due dates from wall-clock time, current ID sort, or unit-number modulo. Proposed scope is every main-path gate Units3–30; Unit2 retains its existing foundation review. Unit1 has no earlier taught objectives. This frequency is an engineering policy choice, subject to independent educational assessment; it is not a quoted normative frequency. Persist policy identity/schema and bind policy declarations into unit/pack content identities and generated review packets.

Each policy needs an explicitly authored ordered pool of earlier objective IDs, with an explicit skill tag and applicable candidate independent check IDs. The final declaration must name actual present objectives/checks from BASELINE_INVENTORY, and its educational scope must be reviewed; do not infer skill from item kind alone or call distinct grammar topics separate modalities. Vocabulary, morphology, syntax, reading and printed-letter recognition may have bounded closed-response evidence. Audio, controlled speaking and handwriting-production skill tags cannot be satisfied by these choices; IA004 foundation limitations remain. No new Russian text, private book prose or new linguistic admission is needed for reusing existing admissible checks.

Bound each assignment to at most six items, one item per distinct selected objective; require at least two distinct eligible objectives and, where the available taught pool spans skills, at least two explicit skill tags. Authored pool priority ensures skill coverage first, then objectives with no recent matching assignment completion, then the recorded last matching review sequence. Deterministic authored priority resolves ties, not item/source/lesson ID order. This is bounded repeated practice of taught supplied checks, not unseen-transfer or productive-mastery evidence.

Eligibility requires: owner is earlier on the declared curriculum path; owner is openable with current instruction/practice/check evidence; candidate is a declared independent check of a required RECOGNITION objective; options/key are valid; current item, owner unit, teaching steps, objective relationships, source/native/context and response-rule identities match. Placement/skipped prerequisites, a bare historical completed-unit flag, a newly open current unit, optional GUIDANCE glosses, earlier memorized correct IDs and stale governed attempts are not taught evidence. Qualified pending source/rights/semantic statuses stay pending; development availability must never mean approval. Unavailable or stale candidates cannot enter a new assignment.

If fewer than two eligible objectives remain, return an explicit insufficient-taught-evidence state with a repair/teach-earlier route. Do not count an empty assignment as complete, silently select untaught checks, or waive the due flag. Resume-from-book users have not supplied governed earlier evidence; show the exact bounded prerequisite review/instruction route needed to make candidates eligible. This is a visible new requirement, not proof of their preexisting ability. The selection and placement policy needs acceptance tests and independent review before activation.

## Immutable assignment and genuine completion

Create a persisted assignment atomically when the learner opens the applicable due-review gate. Read-only progress/Continue computations must not create it. Reopening, an incorrect answer, process restart, optional practice or clock changes must not resample it. Store assignment ID, schema, target gate/unit, policy digest, explicit candidate sequence, owner objective/skill tags, item/unit/teaching/response-rule identity hashes, creation sequence and accepted-order boundary. Assignment identity uses canonical length-prefixed fields, following existing canonicalParts/canonicalText, and a SHA256. A global pack hash may be diagnostic metadata; do not invalidate unrelated assignments solely for an unrelated unit change.

Use a distinct CUMULATIVE_REVIEW attempt role and persisted assignment linkage. Extend preparation/storage/backup role validation together. New review answers never grant prescribed objective, lesson-step or unit-check credit. Conversely prescribed answers, optional lab answers, replayed prior review answers and unordered legacy rows never complete the assignment. A matching answer needs the exact assignment and immutable slot, current governed identities, positive acceptedOrder after assignment creation, a real current learner mutation epoch, and selection equals the governed answer. Use the latest accepted matching answer for each slot: a later wrong answer makes that slot incomplete until corrected. Do not manufacture credit by setting only `correct=true` or copying a prior selection. Existing prescribed history remains intact.

Complete only when every assigned slot has current correct evidence; retain the assignment, all attempts and a completion identity. Stale assignment policy/item/source/teaching relationships must block credit. Preserve a stale assignment as historical evidence, show why it cannot count, and explicitly create a replacement with a new identity and boundary; never mutate or silently retarget its slots. Accepted-order allocation must share a transaction/serialization discipline with assignment creation to prevent answers racing ahead of their assignment boundary. Duplicate taps on the same slot cannot create multiple obligations or grant double credit.

## Persistence, migration, backup and restore

AppDatabase is currently version9. Add additive migration9→10 (or the actual next version if development changes before implementation), registered in both LocalStore database builders. Proposed dedicated tables: assignments, immutable assignment slots, review attempts or links to governed attempts, and completed review identities. Use FK/uniqueness constraints for assignment/slot identity; persist all source identities, not only JSON kept in a composable. If reusing `v2_attempts`, add linkage with an explicit empty legacy default and ensure all existing consumers reject the new role for prescribed credit. Do not use synthetic ordinary step IDs as a substitute for assignment linkage.

Migration preserves every old profile, step, attempt ID/acceptedOrder, note, bookmark, tutor item, attestation, recording and completion. It creates no retroactive assignment/answer/completion, and does not rewrite legacy digests. Old completed units remain historical. Separate evaluation of ordinary taught-objective evidence from the new future review obligation so migrating an already completed unit does not recursively invalidate all its downstream units or erase legitimate old work. Existing completion derivation calls currentUnitRequirements from V2CompletionEvidence.current, so adding a due-review call back to currentCompletedUnitNumbers there would recurse: introduce an acyclic base-evidence evaluator, and test graph/prerequisite failures. Record the policy activation boundary/legacy completion disposition rather than silently grandfathering unproven new review evidence.

Backup currently has strict format/schema version1 and `ignoreUnknownKeys=false`; update its versioned contract explicitly. New exports must include assignments/slots/attempt links/completions and their counts/hashes. Accept old schema1 backups through an explicit decoder/migration that preserves their rows and creates no cumulative completion, or explicitly report unsupported old version before any replacement; preserving old-backup usability is preferred. Validate duplicate identities, missing assignment/slot links, ordering/boundaries, hash consistency, roles, counts and impossible completion claims before destructive replacement. Extend replace, snapshot export, rollback/recovery journal, reset/clear and private-recording handling together. A failed restore must retain the previous learner records; a successful replacement must not accidentally retain another learner's review obligations or in-flight answers. Advance/restore epoch races must fail closed.

## Gate and UI integration

`V2App.onAdvance` already rereads the learner and validates the gate before writing; preserve that protection and add assignment completion to the final serialized transaction. Display policy and remaining slots before the learner attempts advancement (MA2-085). Add a due-review route in Unit detail and Continue after ordinary work, with loading/write failure states and restart-safe resume. Route each error to the existing source-owner remediation, preserving the immutable assignment while returning to teaching. Show insufficient evidence/stale source repair explicitly. Do not claim an unavailable audio/production route has been completed by a text choice.

Leave optional mixed/grammar/vocabulary labs optional. Preserve Unit2's existing foundation-review behavior. Units3–30's existing instruction/book/check minima and unresolved source-capability blockers remain required; review cannot bypass them. Current completed-unit derivation must not reimpose newly activated review obligations on old units without a separately documented, evidence-preserving transition. No change may make pending semantic/rights review or device acceptance count as approved.

## Required correction acceptance scope (not yet run)

1. Otherwise-complete active gate blocked by absent/incomplete due assignment; complete assigned distinct objectives/skills and advance through real UI/store recheck. Preserve Unit2 and ordinary minima/source blockers.
2. Untaught/current/later/skipped/unavailable/optional/stale item or instruction cannot be selected. Explicit insufficient-pool route, including resume placement. Authored order/skills survive shuffled IDs/source-file order.
3. Old correct IDs, prescribed answers, optional answers, other-assignment answers, forged correct flags, pre-boundary/unordered attempts cannot count. Later wrong→correct transitions and duplicate taps use accepted sequence, not timestamps.
4. Restart/offline reopen preserves exact slots/history/completion; clock/restart/optional-practice changes do not resample. Remediation returns to the same assignment.
5. Coherent item/key/unit/source/teaching/policy/assignment mutation rejects old credit; unrelated-unit changes do not invalidate an unaffected assignment. Stale replacement retains original history.
6. Real migration9→10 with populated data retains every original row/order and creates no synthetic review credit; acyclic current completion and migrated old completions remain usable with the new activation boundary.
7. New backup roundtrip preserves assignments and exact answers; old-schema migration, invalid links/hashes/counts, partial restore faults and restore/advance races preserve or atomically replace the correct learner. In-flight pre-restore answers cannot write to restored assignments.
8. All affected rule/content/review/implementation ancestry, source-digest fixtures, readiness/dossier/owner artifacts and current unfrozen candidate refresh coherently. Preserve historical packets/raw results/screenshot evidence and original nine diagnostic hashes.
