====Usage notes====
* Particularly since the 21st century, this conjunction (stressed as ''да́бы'') has re-entered colloquial usage as a more flavorful or intimate synonym to {{l|ru|чтобы}}. This is often seen as improper by older speakers. Historically, ''дабы'' became increasingly archaic following the end of the 18th century, but it remains amply attested in literature.
