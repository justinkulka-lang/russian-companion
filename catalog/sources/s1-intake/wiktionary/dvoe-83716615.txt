====Usage notes====
'''{{m|ru||Дво́е}}''' is used with nouns that only have a plural form (pluralia tantum). It can also be used to denote a number of people. The nominative and accusative cases of a [[collective]] [[numeral]] govern the [[genitive case|genitive]] plural of the noun, pronoun, or adjective. The other cases agree with that of the noun, pronoun, or adjective.

====Usage notes====
{{U:ru:collective numeral}}

Examples:
* In the nominative, governing a genitive plural:
*: {{ux|ru|[[навстречу|Навстре́чу]] [[я|мне]] [[идти|шли]] '''дво́е''' [[парень|парне́й]].|'''Two''' guys came to meet me.}}
*: {{ux|ru|[[у|У]] [[я|меня́]] [[найтись|найдётся]] '''дво́е''' [[ножницы|но́жниц]].|I have '''two''' (pairs of) scissors.|lit=With me are '''two''' (pairs of) scissors.}}
* In the accusative, governing a genitive plural when meaning "a pair of":
*: {{ux|ru|[[надо|На́до]] [[взять]] [[с]] [[себя|собо́й]] '''дво́е''' [[носок|носко́в]].|You need to take '''a pair of''' socks with you.}}
* In the accusative, governing an accusative plural when meaning "two":
*: {{ux|ru|[[я|Я]] [[там]] [[встретить|встре́тил]] '''двои́х''' [[сослуживец|сослужи́вцев]].|I met '''two''' of my colleagues there.}}
